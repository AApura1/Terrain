The normal movement settings are used when the player is not on any special terrain, providing a responsive baseline to compare the three terrain types.
For the Sticky terrain, I reduced the player's speed and increased the time needed to accelerate and stop and this makes the character feel slower and heavier.
For the Icy terrain, I increased the player's maximum speed and made the stopping time much longer. This makes the character slide and become harder to control.
For the Windy terrain, I used a moderate movement speed with quick acceleration and added a wind force that pushes the player to the right.
